Train in practical cake baking and decoration with flexible programme lengths from quick skills sessions to full-course training.

Training Packages & Fees

  • 1 Week — Ksh 9,500: Basics of either Baking or Decorations.
  • 2 Weeks — Ksh 17,500: Basics of both Baking & Decorations.
  • 3 Weeks — Ksh 35,000: Half Course.
  • 8 Weeks — Ksh 60,000: Full Course.

4–5 hours of practical training daily.

What You Learn

  • Cake baking fundamentals and flavouring
  • Icing, finishing, piping and writing
  • Spongy cakes and decoration
  • Fondant work and cake finishing
  • Cupcakes, muffins and pastries
  • Floral work and creative cake decoration

What Is Provided & Requirements

  • All ingredients, tools and equipment are provided.
  • For shorter packages, trainees bring their own apron, notebook and pen.
  • The Full Course provides an apron, notebook and pen free of charge.
  • All baked goods made during training are carried home by the trainee.
  • A certificate on the skills learned is issued.
